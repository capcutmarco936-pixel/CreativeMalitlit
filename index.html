Angle & Mechanism

Our Process

The playbook,
simplified.

Funnel-first. Research-first. Learnings-first. Here's the exact sequence that takes brands from stuck to scaling.

1

✍️ Fix the funnel before the ads

We audit where your traffic lands first. A great ad hitting a broken page loses every time. Get conversion rate × AOV to a number that clears your CPC — then we scale.

2

🔬 We become your customer

We download your buyer's beliefs, map their identity, study what they've already tried, and identify the angle most likely to make them stop scrolling and start reading.

3

🚀 Receive creatives & start testing

Finished video ads and static ads delivered in 5 business days. Ready to run. Revisions included until you're 100% satisfied.

4

📈 Run learnings, compound the output

We study every ad that runs — not surface reads like "it's UGC," but real component-level learnings from the customer's POV. Winners get iterated. Lanes get proven. Each batch beats the last.
